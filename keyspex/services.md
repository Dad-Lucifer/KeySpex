## A. Website & Business Solutions

*Look credible. Stay organised.*

### 01. Business Portfolio Website

**For:** Businesses that are hard to find, or hard to trust, online.

A professional website that presents your services, work and contact details in one credible place, so potential customers can learn about you before they get in touch.

- Services and portfolio shown clearly
- Contact details in one place
- A layout that works on phones and laptops

**Benefit:** A clear, credible first impression.

*Not sure what your website should include? Ask us.*

### 02. Business Dashboard

**For:** Businesses whose data is scattered across sheets and notes.

A digital interface that organises your business data and lets you monitor the metrics you choose to track. Features are agreed as part of your project scope.

- Data organised in one place
- Selected metrics shown clearly
- Easier review and reporting

**Benefit:** Better visibility to support everyday decisions.

*Tell us which numbers matter most to you.*

### 03. Store Management System *(for offline stores)*

**For:** Shop owners who track stock and sales by hand.

A digital system that helps offline store owners move away from manual records and keep inventory and revenue information in one place.

> **Note:** This is an internal tool for running your shop, not a customer-facing online store.

- A more organised view of your inventory
- Digital tracking of relevant stock information
- Visibility into recorded sales and revenue
- Easier review of store records and performance

**Benefit:** A clearer picture of your stock and sales.

Exact features are agreed based on your store's needs.

*Running an offline store? Let's talk about what you track today.*
